## The entry

> **Noddrift (n.): nodding along to a conversation you stopped actually hearing minutes ago.**

A new English word for a moment everyone knows: your head keeps nodding, the other person keeps talking, and your mind left minutes ago. *Nod* + *drift* — sayable, spellable, and instantly usable.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the single-line entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
